Before you claim

  • Check the host — every card shows the official domain at the bottom. A faucet never asks for your recovery phrase.
  • Watch the network — the same faucet can switch between mainnet and testnet: read the selector before you confirm.
  • The Stakely flow — address, captcha, then a public share of the request ID on X. No wallet connection, no staking.
  • Expect very little — a mainnet faucet sends a fraction of a token to cover gas, not a usable balance.
